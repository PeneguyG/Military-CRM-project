You are ready to open the CRM

  1. Click Close to finish installing.
  2. Open Finder (the smiling face in your Dock).
  3. Click Applications in the sidebar.
  4. Double-click Steel Wings CRM.

At sign-in, use the CRM password supplied by your administrator. If you previously changed it, use your existing password. After your first sign-in, open Settings → Change password to set your own password.

To keep the app handy, right-click its Dock icon and choose Options → Keep in Dock.

Local records work offline. Online maps and external services need an internet connection. Keep your installer if you need to reinstall later.
