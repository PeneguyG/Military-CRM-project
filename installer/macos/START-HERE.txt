STEEL WINGS CRM — INSTALL ON A MAC

You can install from a USB drive or a downloaded copy. No internet or technical
tools are needed during installation. Requires macOS 12 or later.

1. Save your work and quit Steel Wings CRM if it is open.
2. Double-click the file named Steel-Wings-CRM followed by a version and .pkg.
3. Click Continue, then Install. If asked, approve with your Mac login password
   or Touch ID. This is not your CRM password.
4. When installation finishes, click Close.
5. Open Finder (the smiling face in your Dock), click Applications, and
   double-click Steel Wings CRM.
6. Sign in with the CRM password supplied by your administrator. If you already
   changed your password, use that password. You can change it in Settings.

KEEP IT IN YOUR DOCK
While the CRM is open, right-click its Dock icon and choose Options, then
Keep in Dock. Next time, click that icon to open it.

OFFLINE USE
Your local records work without internet. Online map tiles and external services
need a connection. The installer includes the app and its required libraries.

UPDATING
Back up important records and close the CRM before running a newer installer.
Your records and saved password are stored separately from the app. Keep a copy
of the installer if you need it again. Do not delete your database or Keychain
entries to troubleshoot installation.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
If your Mac blocks the installer or says its developer cannot be verified, ask
the person who supplied it for an Apple-signed and notarized copy. Do not turn
off your Mac's security settings.
If installation asks for an administrator account you do not have, contact the
person who manages your Mac. If sign-in fails, ask your CRM administrator for
the correct password. Reinstalling does not reset a saved password.
