Welcome to Steel Wings CRM

This installer puts Steel Wings CRM in your Applications folder. You do not need Terminal, Flutter, or an internet connection to install it.

Before you begin: Save your work and quit Steel Wings CRM if it is already open. You need macOS 12 or later and about 250 MB of free space.

Click Continue, then Install. Your Mac may ask for your Mac login password or Touch ID to approve installation. This is separate from your CRM password.

Updating an existing installation replaces the app. Your CRM records and saved password are stored separately. Back up important records before updating.
